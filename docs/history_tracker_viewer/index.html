Support page for the History Tracker userscript

This page is empty on purpose. With the userscript installed and enabled in Tampermonkey, your saved history opens here, so you can browse it even when the sites themselves are unavailable.

Seeing this message instead? The userscript isn't installed, is turned off, or is older than v3.4. Nothing is stored on this page or sent anywhere.
